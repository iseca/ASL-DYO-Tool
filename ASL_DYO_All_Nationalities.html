Built from ASL Chapter H — German/Russian plus U.S., British, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, French, Allied Minor, Axis Minor, Finnish, and Korean-War (UN/Communist) sections (© Multi-Man Publishing, LLC — data reproduced for personal use per the Chapter H photocopy grant). U.S., Finnish, Japanese and Chinese listings were OCR-transcribed; verify unusual values against your chapter. Korean-War infantry BPVs are not in Chapter H — edit them from the Forgotten War chart. Monthly Rarity Factors (from the Nationality Vehicle RF spreadsheet) supersede listing RFs for 680 matched vehicles, including per-area variants (e.g., [PTO], [vs Russians]); vehicles without a monthly table use the printed listing RF. Ordnance monthly RFs integrated (447 of 466 gun rows matched; “a” months = allotted-as-SW, conditional PTO invasion rows flagged). Free French British/U.S.-supplied equipment appears as extra browsers with France (Free) RFs. Korean War monthly RFs are per-force (U.S. Army, U.S.M.C., ROKA, KMC, OUNC, BCFK; KPA, CPVA) and follow the Force selector; units without a row for the selected force are shown as not fielded. Purchase sequence, costs and charts per H1.1–1.84. Infantry BPVs per the A25 National Capabilities Chart (editable in the Infantry panel — verify against your chart). † RF values that vary by date use the listed range; the app checks the most favorable value and flags the entry — consult the pertinent Vehicle/Ordnance Note. Optional armament BPV equals the weapon's IFT FP (H1.41) and is entered manually per the pertinent Note. SW & leaders are allotted, not purchased (H1.8–.83).
